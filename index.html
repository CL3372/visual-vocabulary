Learn vocabulary with real photos

LexPix is a visual vocabulary app that is free to start. See a real photo, hear the word and learn it in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Russian and many more languages.

Browse vocabulary topics Get it on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How LexPix works

  1. Pick a topic, such as animals, verbs, emotions or the food of a country.
  2. Choose the language you want to learn and see each word with a real photo.
  3. Practise with flashcards and quizzes, and keep a daily streak going.

New to learning words with pictures? Read what visual vocabulary is, or follow a simple method to learn words with pictures.
